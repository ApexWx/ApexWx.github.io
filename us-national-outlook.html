Thanksgiving Storm Departs as New System Brings Snow, Rain, and Thunderstorms

The powerful Thanksgiving storm gradually weakens Saturday, although lingering rain across northern New England and wet snow in the central Appalachian mountains continue into the morning. Gusty winds persist across much of the eastern United States before lighter winds and warmer temperatures bring improving conditions Sunday.

Meanwhile, a Pacific storm spreads mountain snow across the northern and central Rockies Saturday, with wet snow extending into the nearby High Plains. As the system strengthens and moves eastward, showers, thunderstorms, and increasingly gusty southerly winds develop across the Deep South Sunday.

By Sunday night into Monday morning, rain reaches the East Coast, with locally heavy rainfall and possible thunderstorms across the Mid-Atlantic. The storm tracks from the middle Mississippi Valley toward the lower Great Lakes, producing soaking rain before strong northwest winds drive colder air eastward and deep into the South.

Another Pacific storm approaches the West Coast late Sunday, spreading rain into Oregon and northern California. Locally heavy rainfall becomes possible in northern California by Monday morning, while strengthening southerly winds affect coastal areas and higher elevations.
