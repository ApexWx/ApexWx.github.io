Heavy Rain, Localized Flooding, and Snow Across Several Regions

A low-pressure system moving northward from the Southeast toward southeastern Canada brings heavy rain to parts of New England Sunday into Monday. Localized flash flooding threatens urban areas, roads, small streams, and low-lying locations. Scattered thunderstorms develop across the Southeast, while additional showers reach southern Florida Monday.

Another front spreads rain from the Upper Midwest into the Mississippi Valley, while colder air produces snow showers across Michigan's Upper Peninsula and northern Lower Peninsula overnight Sunday into Monday.

Along the western Gulf Coast, thunderstorms bring heavy rainfall and localized flash-flooding concerns through Monday morning. Meanwhile, high pressure supports freezing temperatures across portions of the Southwest, with freeze alerts affecting New Mexico, Arizona, and western Texas. Dense fog also develops along portions of the Gulf Coast and Lower Mississippi Valley.

Across the West, a Pacific front produces rain and mountain snow from the Northwest into northern California and the Rockies. Snow reaches the Sierra Nevada Sunday, followed by additional mountain snow and lower-elevation rain across the central and southern Rockies Monday.
