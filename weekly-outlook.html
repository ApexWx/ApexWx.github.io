Short Term: Friday, November 27 — Sunday, November 29

Lingering moisture and a relatively tight pressure gradient maintain scattered showers and gusty southwest winds on Friday, though rainfall amounts remain light. As the system departs, drier air spreads into the Lower Susquehanna Valley, allowing high pressure to restore settled conditions through much of the weekend.

A weakening pressure gradient and decreasing cloud cover favor brisk nighttime cooling Saturday night, particularly in sheltered rural valleys. Relatively mild daytime temperatures persist as high pressure shifts eastward, allowing southerly flow to develop ahead of another approaching disturbance. Increasing moisture supports a 90% chance of rain showers Sunday night with modest rainfall totals, though exact onset timing and localized amounts remain the primary uncertainties.

Increasing cloud cover midweek temporarily tempers overnight cooling before temperatures climb well above early-December averages by Thursday, with no significant precipitation or wind hazards expected through the period.

While an active storm track increases the potential for wetter periods, individual system timing remains uncertain. Typical temperatures for this stretch feature daytime highs in the upper 40s to near 50°F and overnight lows in the lower to mid-30s.
