Lingering west winds diminish as the pressure gradient weakens, while decreasing cloud cover favors substantial nighttime cooling, particularly in sheltered rural locations. Another strengthening low-pressure system then approaches from the Mississippi Valley, drawing warmer, moisture-rich air northward ahead of an advancing cold front. Colder air follows Monday night, introducing localized refreezing concerns where residual moisture persists. The timing of the strongest winds remains the primary uncertainty.
